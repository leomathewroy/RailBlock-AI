Key Patterns to Discover

• Train type hierarchy: Vande Bharat and Rajdhani get infrastructure priority, their delay rate is dramatically lower than Mail/Express or Passenger trains even on the same routes

• Zone congestion: Eastern Railway (ER) and Northern Railway (NR) are chronically congested, nearly 90%+ capacity utilisation creates cascading delays.

• Monsoon effect: July–August see the highest delay rates in the dataset. Flooding disrupts track in SER and ECoR zones; signal failures spike across all zones.

• Fog cascade: December–February morning departures from fog-prone zones (NR, NER, NCR) see delay rates 2–3× the annual average.

• Late incoming rake: When the previous service on the same rake is delayed, the probability of the next service being delayed exceeds 80%

• LHB coaches: Trains with modern LHB (Linke-Hofmann-Busch) coaches have significantly better punctuality than ICF coach trains on equivalent routes.

Important Notes

Leakage Warning

The columns primary_delay_cause and delay_minutes are present in the training set only. They are NOT available in the test set and should not be used as features. Using them on the training set would constitute target leakage and your model will fail on test data.
